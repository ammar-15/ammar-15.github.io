# Catch Up

A lot has happened since I last blogged.

I quit **Walmart**, joined **Holiday Inn** as a night auditor, and then left that to move to **Toronto**.

## Winter Refused to Die

The winter weather somehow seeped into **June**. It took until the end of June for me to finally stop using my furnace, but those few cold days made me refill my oil tank anyway, just in case winter decided *not* to leave.

## Marketing, But Make It Human

I visited **HeyOrca's Community-Led Marketing Summit** and learned a lot about their community-led marketing approach.

How much better it is to have a **human connection** at the centre of your product's marketing. You could have an *insanely good product*, but **no one will buy it if it's not packaged and presented properly.**

Seeing marketers share their stories on stage and talking to them afterwards helped me *shift my approach as well*.

## Back Behind the Front Desk

As summer arrived in **July**, I got hired as a night auditor at the **Holiday Inn St. John's Conference Centre**.

**OPERA PMS** was easy to master, and working at a hotel *felt like home again*.

Around the same time, a new event in the city popped up for designers that intrigued me.

The brief was simple:

**Make a poster for a design event built around creativity, feedback, and growth.**

I wanted to focus on the *feeling* of critique. Because honestly, **feedback can feel scary.** Especially when you're someone who used to be shy.

Even when the feedback is helpful, there's still that moment where your work is just sitting there, **exposed**.

So I built the poster around *that feeling*.

## The Feeling That Wouldn't Go Away

**August arrived, and summer stayed.**

The city was lively, with lots of events and places to be. But something in me *wasn't ready to settle for this*.

**I wanted more.**

***I want to reach greater heights.***

And I felt like that wouldn't be possible if I stayed in **St. John's**, because the city felt constricting in terms of opportunities.

Of course, moving to a big city comes with **more opportunities**, along with a lot more competition. But I had a gut feeling that I needed to take that **leap of faith**.

I spoke with a couple of my friends who are devs just to get a second opinion, and they all hinted at the same thing:

***Move to a bigger city.***

The more I looked at it, the more it made sense to move. **A chance for a better life for myself and my wife.**

Hence, after much consultation with myself, my wife, and my friends, **I booked my flight.**

## Leaving Home

Leaving the place that moulded me into the man I am today, from the **shy 18-year-old kid** who started university **10,000 km away from India**, was truly sad.

Yes, **St. John's** has its pros and cons. No city is perfect.

But it was ***my home***.

And for better opportunities, I had to take that **leap of faith**.

Just like that quote from *A Most Violent Year*:

> *"When it feels scary to jump, that is exactly when you jump, otherwise you end up staying in the same place your whole life, and that I can't do."*

And just like that...

***Hello, Toronto!***
